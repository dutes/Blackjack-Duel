Welcome to Blackjack Duel

This is a guided tutorial that will walk you through a single round of Blackjack Duel, a head-to-head Blackjack game.

The rules are simple: scoring is the same as normal blackjack, best of three hands. The new element is the hidden bust.

You will not know if your opponent has busted. You need to get the hand total closest to 21 before standing. Closest hand wins the game point.

Scoring

  • Win: 1 point
  • Push: 0 points
  • Loss: 0 points

Player with highest score after 3 hands wins the round.

There are some extra elements that the tutorial will walk you through.
