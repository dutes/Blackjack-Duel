Blackjack Duel

Play the player, not the house.

1v1 Duel The MVP: two players head-to-head, or a guided tutorial against a bot. Open it in two tabs to play yourself. The free server can take about a minute to wake up. King of the Hill An 8-player knockout tournament against bot opponents, each with its own strategy. First prototype (July 2025) Where it started: one HTML file, three hands and a Redraw against an AI.
